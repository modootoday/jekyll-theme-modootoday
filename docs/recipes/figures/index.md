# Figures and widgets

These recipes turn Markdown that GitHub already understands (a Mermaid or math fence, a table) into figures on your site, and place custom elements with a text fallback. Each one works under a strict CSP.

<!-- spec:docs-index:begin -->
- [Math and diagrams](math-diagrams/) — Show Mermaid diagrams and math as static SVG and MathML you commit, or render them with the built-in loaders or your own, and the cost to CSP and privacy.
- [Diagram and math loaders](loaders/) — Render Mermaid diagrams and KaTeX math in the browser with the built-in loaders or your own, and what each one asks of your CSP and privacy.
- [Charts](charts/) — Turn a Markdown table into a chart figure, or show a data file as one, with the table as the text every reader gets.
- [Widgets](widgets/) — Place your own custom element in a page from Markdown, with text that readers without JavaScript get instead.
