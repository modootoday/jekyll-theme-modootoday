# Post demo

The live demo is a post of the blog starter. `layout: post` is what a file in `_posts` gets by default: above the text it prints the title, a deck, the byline, the dates, the reading time and the cover; below it, the series, tags and categories, share links, the previous and next posts and related posts, with `BlogPosting` structured data.

[Blog and news](../) lists the front matter of the post layout, with author data and feeds.
