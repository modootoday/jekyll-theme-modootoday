# Pricing demo

The live demo is the pricing page of the landing starter, a `layout: pricing` page with one card per plan: a Monthly/Yearly switch made of two radio buttons changes the prices with CSS alone, and the recommended plan is marked in text.

[Marketing pages](../) lists the front matter of the pricing layout and of the other marketing layouts.
